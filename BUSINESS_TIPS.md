# Pricing & Business Guide for Wincer Cake House

## 1. Professional Quote Template
Copy and adapt this email/WhatsApp message:

> "Hi [Owner Name],
>
> I've been working on the Wincer Cake House platform and it's coming along great! I’ve implemented the automated M-Pesa checkout, the WhatsApp order flow, and a custom dashboard so you can update your own cakes.
>
> To move forward with the final launch and domain setup, here is my proposal for the project:
>
> **Project Scope:**
> - Full E-commerce Catalog & Mobile-Friendly Design
> - Integrated M-Pesa Payment System
> - Admin Panel for Price/Stock Management
> - Technical Support & Domain Setup
>
> **Investment:** [Insert Total, e.g., 50,000 Kes]
>
> **Flexible Payment Options:**
> Since I'm focused on seeing your business grow, I'm happy to offer a split payment:
> - **Deposit:** 30% to begin final deployment.
> - **Final:** 70% once the site is live and you are satisfied.
>
> (Optional: Or we can do a monthly plan of [Amount] over 6 months if that suits your current cash flow better.)
>
> Looking forward to your thoughts!"

## 2. Dealing with "Low Expectations"
If they say "I thought it would be cheaper/free because you're a student," respond with:
- *"I understand! While I am a student, I am delivering a professional tool that saves you time on manual WhatsApp replies and automates your payments. This site is an investment that will pay for itself in just a few cake sales."*

## 3. Don't Forget Recurring Income
Even after they pay for the site, you should charge a small "Management Fee" (e.g., 2,000 Kes/month). This covers:
- Keeping the server running (Firebase).
- Minor text or price updates.
- Technical support if something breaks.

## 4. The "Kill Switch" (Safety)
**Never** transfer the domain name or give them full Admin access to the Firebase project until the final payment is made. Keep the "keys" until you have the cash!

## 5. Long-Term Involvement (Post-Launch)
The site will need you even after it's live. Here is how to handle recurring tasks:

### Technical Tasks only you can do:
- **API Updates**: M-Pesa (Daraja) or Maps updates.
- **Bug Fixes**: Handling browser updates or library security patches.
- **Structural Changes**: Adding new pages or changing the layout for holidays.

### How to charge for it:
Propose a **Monthly Maintenance Retainer**:
- **Rate**: 2,000 - 5,000 Kes/month.
- **Includes**: 1-2 hours of small updates + "Emergency" support if the site goes down.
- **Excludes**: New major features (these should be billed as separate "mini-projects").

### Why this is good for YOU:
- It builds your portfolio with a "long-term client."
- It provides steady, predictable income while you are a student.
- It ensures the site doesn't "rot" and look bad later, which would hurt your reputation.
